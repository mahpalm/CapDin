The Founder

Chef Mahmud

"A pot of Afang doesn't lie about where it was cooked. Ours is cooked at home."

Chef Mahmud trained in kitchens along the Delta coastline before returning to Benin City to open a dining room that treats South-South cooking as the fine cuisine it has always been. Every soup on this menu is pounded, ground and simmered the way it would be in an Uyo kitchen, a Warri compound, or an Edo family house on a Sunday.

The restaurant itself sits on Airport Road, its interior detailing borrowed from Benin's bronze-casting tradition — a nod to the guild of artisans that once cast plaques for the Oba's palace, a few kilometres from where we now cast our own pepper soup.
